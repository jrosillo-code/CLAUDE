# Research: what the best sites in this field look like, and the design brief

*Researched 12 September 2026 from about 60 searches. Direct page loads were blocked, so
visual descriptions come from design galleries, agency case studies and press; headline
copy marked VERIFIED appeared in page titles or on-page text in search results.*

## The gap the site should own

Everyone in the field sells "AI agents built specifically for X". Nobody sells
"operaciones con revisión humana y trazabilidad" for Spanish corredurías and asesorías with
a visible price, a visible timeline and a visible review screen. Own that.

## Shared patterns

- Headline formula: "[AI agents or platform] built specifically for [persona]" plus one
  outcome. "Specifically" and "native" are the category's tics.
- Subhead names the drudgery and promises time back, often with a number.
- Section order: hero with demo CTA and product image; logo strip or big numbers; how it
  works in three steps; feature bento; integrations; security and compliance; case study
  quotes with numbers; final CTA. Pricing pages are absent in vertical AI and present in
  horizontal tools and Spanish SMB software.
- CTA: "book a demo" is universal in vertical AI; Spanish sites say "solicita una demo"
  or "reserva 15 minutos con un consultor".
- Trust stack: SOC 2 plus ISO 27001 plus GDPR or HIPAA, top-N logos, one aggregate number,
  investor names, and an explicit "responsible AI, you stay in control" page.
- Human-in-the-loop as copy: "from doing to reviewing", "drafts but never sends", "ability
  to intervene". Review, not replacement, is the safe promise.
- Visuals: light UI with real product screenshots dominates vertical AI; dark-and-neon is
  for developer tools. Brand-led exceptions use one typographic idea.
- Localization: the English cohort is English only; Afori is German first; Spanish
  incumbents are Spanish first with regional languages.

## Where they all look the same, and 2026 trends

Sameness to exploit: interchangeable headlines; a US badge stack a solo founder cannot
reproduce and Spanish SMB buyers do not read; opaque "book a demo" with no price, scope or
timeline; abstract "agents" language with the review screen hidden; Spanish incumbents
with dense feature lists, stock photos and event feeds.

Trends per galleries and design press (REPORTED): the product is the demo, shown working
in the hero; two dominant aesthetics, techno-futurist dark and editorial cream, with the
AI wave pushing restraint; monochrome plus one accent used only where money moves (Ramp)
or for focus (Attio); typography as identity (Harvey, Mercury, Ramp); bento grids for
multi-feature products; "boring is trust" in regulated markets, with numbers, regulatory
information and the override path as hero content; plainly structured factual content
because buyers ask assistants for shortlists; human-review UI conventions where approve
or reject is the only executing action.

## Spanish-market specifics

- Regulatory vocabulary is the trust signal, not SOC 2: "homologado por la Agencia
  Tributaria", "Verifactu incluido", EIAC, DGSFP, "obligatorio desde 1 enero 2027".
- Colegios de Mediadores are the distribution and legitimacy channel: co-branded pages,
  Consejo General discounts, "entidades colaboradoras" lists.
- Kit Digital and Kit Consulting have mandated web elements for accredited providers;
  Kit Consulting may fit a services-first founder better (verify eligibility).
- WhatsApp is a product surface, not just a CTA; a WhatsApp button reads as native.
- The sales motion is consultative and human: a named person, a phone number, "reserva 15
  minutos".
- Tone: tú with professional vocabulary is the sector norm for software; usted survives
  only in insurer and legal pages.
- Pricing transparency is unusual but not alien (Mediator, Foliume, Javadex, accounting
  tools).
- Data residency phrasing: "datos alojados en la UE", RGPD, ISO 27001 via AENOR.
- Catalan matters for Barcelona brokerages; English is for investors, not buyers.

## Design brief

Positioning line: "Tus operaciones, con IA y con control."

Section order with copy:
1. Hero, product first, light: the real review-queue UI with one item open, source text
   highlighted, extracted fields with their quotes, two missing items as tasks, approve
   and correct actions, the audit line beneath. CTA "Pide una auditoría de 30 minutos";
   secondary "Ver la cola de revisión"; WhatsApp and phone in the nav.
2. Qué hacemos: bento of five tiles; heading "Cinco pasos que hoy hace tu equipo a mano."
3. Cómo funciona: "Llega el documento. La IA propone. Tu equipo aprueba."
4. Con qué trabajamos: two columns naming the ERPs.
5. Seguridad y cumplimiento: the Spanish stack (RGPD and DPA, datos en la UE, no
   training, Article 50 disclosure, human oversight, exportable audit log, Verifactu and
   SII readiness through a certified engine). ISO 27001 only when held.
6. Precios: three cards with public bands and durations; "si en el sprint no ahorramos
   horas medibles, no seguimos".
7. Resultados: pre-customer version shows the method and the three metrics that will be
   measured; no fake logos.
8. Quién está detrás: the founder, named, with sector credentials.
9. Preguntas frecuentes, opening with "¿Qué pasa cuando la IA se equivoca?".
10. Final CTA: "Cuéntame tu flujo más lento. Te digo en 30 minutos si se puede automatizar
    y cuánto costaría." Footer with legal entity, NIF, aviso legal, privacidad, DPA,
    language switch.

Visual direction: paper-white ground, near-black ink, hairline rules, one accent (deep
green) used only on the approve action and live states; an editorial serif for headings
and a humanist grotesque for reading, mono for labels and figures; real UI and document
crops only, no stock, no 3D, no gradients; single column with one bento block; the
WhatsApp and phone CTA sticky on mobile.

Five deliberate differences from the incumbents: show the review screen in the hero;
publish price bands and timelines; replace the US badge stack with the Spanish regulatory
stack and a real trust page; name the failure mode as a section; editorial restraint
instead of ERP density or AI gloss.
